# Competitive Landscape

We are entering three separate markets, each with different competitors, buyers,
and economics. Pricing is approximate (2026) and changes often.

> **See also:** [`competitive-landscape-2026.md`](./competitive-landscape-2026.md) —

> for the elder-care wedge.

## Battleground 1 — Family organization

The "shared calendar + reminders + tasks" space; cheap, mature, now being
re-fought on "AI reads your email and fills the calendar."

| Product | Type | Price | Threat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cozi | App | $39/yr Gold | Incumbent mindshare |
| Apple / Google | Built-in | Free | Default option; hard to beat on price |
| Skylight | Wall display + app | $150–630 + $79/yr | Owns the "wall display" niche |
| Hearth | Wall display + app | $699 + $86/yr | Premium, niche |
| Maple | App | Free / $40/yr | Closest to our v1 family vision |
| SuperDuper | App (2026) | Free / paid | Building the "AI fills your calendar" wedge directly |

**Takeaway:** low willingness-to-pay, high churn; differentiator is being built
by others and is a free feature for Big Tech. Viral funnel, weak standalone.

## Battleground 2 — Elder care (our wedge)

Aging-in-place monitoring + caregiver dashboard. Payer is the adult child.

| Product | Approach | Price | Buyer | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CarePredict | Wearable + room beacons | $499 kit + $70/mo | Adult child | Requires proprietary wearable |
| Sensi.ai | Audio sensor + AI | Custom / B2B | Home-care agencies | Sells to agencies, not families |
| Lively | Simplified phone + alerts | $25–35/mo | Senior + family | Device-centric, thin AI |
| Papa | Human companions | Via health plans | Health plans / employers | Not a monitoring/software play |
| I'm Alive | Daily check-in app | Free | Adult child | Bare-bones; no home/health intelligence |
| Apple Watch | Fall detect / SOS | Free w/ device | Senior | No caregiver dashboard or trend AI |

**Takeaway:** proven willingness-to-pay, motivated buyer, weak software/AI from
hardware-first incumbents, almost no Big-Tech competition. Our edge: software-
only, no-wearable, AI wellness briefing for the adult child, on existing devices.

## Battleground 3 — Smart-home intelligence layer

The most dangerous arena to enter head-on.

| Player | Model | Price | Why hard to beat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home Assistant | Open source, local-first | Free | Already = our exact pitch; now ships LLM "Assist" + voice hardware |
| Apple / Google / Alexa | Platform + devices | Free | Own the OS, speakers, and defaults |
| Josh.ai | Luxury integrator | Premium install | Locked-in high-end niche |
| SmartThings | Samsung ecosystem | Free | Bundled with Samsung devices |
| Hubitat | Local hub | Hardware | Privacy/local crowd already served |

**Takeaway:** "no hardware, reuse devices, local-first, private, unified
intelligence + LLM" is essentially Home Assistant's 2024–2026 roadmap — and it's
free. Build **on top of** HA for plumbing; differentiate on consumer-grade UX for
non-tinkerers.

## 2026 new entrants (deep-research update)

The category exploded in the first half of 2026. See the interactive canvas
`AI-enabled-life-2026-new-entrants.canvas.tsx` for the full teardown. Headlines:

**Family / household AI "OS" (now a red ocean).** A dozen-plus near-identical
launches: Nori / Domus Next (Jan 2026, 100k+ beta families, ex-ByteDance/Samsung,
Family Hub hardware June 2026), Ohai.ai (most full-featured; email/PDF scan, meals
+ Instacart, Circles; free–$29.99/mo), Fynn ("AI Family OS"; $299/yr), Kivu, Rosie
AI (8 modules incl. money/health/smart-home), Vela (voice-first, May 2026),
NestGenie (SMS/voice chief-of-staff), plus familymind/Goldee/Klonr/Peggy/Sense. In
India, **M** (Curious Digital, Kabeer Biswas) raised $11M seed (Peak XV, Blume,
CRED) for an AI household concierge. **Hint** (Martha Stewart, $10M seed, Slow
Ventures) targets proactive home maintenance.

**Two notable shutdowns (Jan 2026): Milo** (OpenAI-backed SMS coordinator, YC
status now inactive) and **Yohana** (Panasonic's human+AI concierge, closed in
Japan) — founders cited the tech not yet being trustworthy enough. Lesson: scope
tightly; don't over-promise "do-anything" concierge.

**Elder care — wedge validated AND crowding.** Our exact model (camera-free,
no-wearable radar + AI briefing for the adult child) now has direct competitors:
**OdeCare** (60GHz wall radar, falls/inactivity/gas/temp, family app),
**Reflekt** (camera-free "pre-crisis" behavior-change intelligence, pilot May–Jul
2026), **Pontosense Silver Shield** (mmWave radar fall detection). Camera-based:
**KamiCare** (Kami Vision, 99.5% fall detection, consumer beta from CES 2026, +
smart ring), **Hello Ello** Vision One (SG/Malaysia). Funding signals: **Patronus**
€11M (3TS Capital) AI companions, **Adaptive Innovations** $50M Series A (Felicis)
AI-native home health. Implication: the radar+briefing angle alone is no longer
differentiated — we need a defensible edge (specific population, distribution
channel, vertical depth, or trust/human-in-the-loop).

**AI home platforms at CES 2026:** TENET (Home Autonomy OS), IRVINEi OVAL ("first
AI Home Hub," ships Apr 2026), LG CLOiD + ThinQ ("Zero Labor Home"), Tuya AI Agent
platform — reinforces that the smart-home layer is being contested by giants.
